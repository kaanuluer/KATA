Your agents

Sign in with a passkey on this device, or with the email bound to your pseudonymous principal id. After the email link signs you in, create a passkey on this device from Account. You can see your agents, their assurance level, their authorizations, who inquired about them, and the incidents filed against them. You can revoke an agent or an intent, and you can dispute an incident. Revoke and dispute ask for the passkey again.

What is stored

  • The email address used to sign in, and a pseudonymous principal id.
  • For each registered device: a credential id, the public half of the passkey, a sign counter, a label, and the time it was added.
  • A browser push address, and the keys the browser issued for that address, only after explicit consent.
  • A short-lived approval record bound to one action, only while approval notices are on. The action text is removed when the approval is used or expires.
  • Session tokens are stored as hashes.

This portal does not store a trust score for you, and it does not score individuals.

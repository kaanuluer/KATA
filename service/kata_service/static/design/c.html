    Dispute evidence

    What the dispute keeps

    Evidence stays with the agent: the signed intent, the payment, and the outcome. A report that is not linked to an agent is rejected. An inquiry uses a purpose code and does not change the score.

    Linked to shopper-01

    The laptop payment at techstore.example, the CAD 1,500 ceiling, and the outcome.

    No agent, no record

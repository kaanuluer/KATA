Know the agent before the payment goes through.

KATA is a framework for knowing who an AI agent is, who authorized it, what it was authorized to do, and whether the action it is about to take still matches that authorization. A valid identity is not the same thing as a valid action.

  1. 01 Who is the agent?

    Identity and provenance of the agent that wants to act.

  2. 02 Who authorized it?

    The principal who signed the delegation and the intent.

  3. 03 What was it authorized to do?

    Purpose, ceiling, currency, merchants, and expiry.

  4. 04 Does this behavior match?

    The payment is checked against that signed record.
